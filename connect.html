Open QuickBooks connection
  1. Sign in to the local workspace if prompted, then open Connection settings → Live Norway company.
  2. Save your Intuit Production settings locally. Follow the return-address instructions shown in the app.
  3. Click Connect live QuickBooks and sign in with Intuit. Select the actual Norway entity.
  4. Finish the authorization in the local app. Check the returned company name, legal name, company ID, country and home currency. Country must be NO.
  5. Confirm the Norway company and import a short date range to compare with QuickBooks.

For reconnection, expand Manage live connection in the local app and use Connect live QuickBooks again.

Live reads only. Intuit's accounting permission is broad; the connector uses production accounting reads only. This connection cannot submit real documents to Billit or Peppol.
