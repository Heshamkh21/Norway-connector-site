1. Purpose and authorised use

These terms cover the DarkVision Norway connector, a locally installed internal finance application operated by DarkVision Technologies Inc NUF. Authorised company personnel may install and use it for internal invoice review, data comparison and explicitly selected sandbox tests, subject to company access rules. Users may access only companies and records they are authorised to use. These terms do not grant rights to third-party software or services.

2. Current functionality

The production QuickBooks connection reads the verified Norway company's identity, relevant preferences, invoices and credit notes for comparison. The connector does not create, change, delete, email or submit real accounting documents. Intuit grants a broad accounting permission; the connector enforces the restriction to production accounting reads in its own code.

Separate generated fictional documents may be created in QuickBooks sandbox and, after explicit user confirmation, copied or sent through Billit/Peppol sandbox to the fixed test receiver. Billit production sending is not enabled. Real imported documents are blocked from these test-copy and submission actions.

3. User responsibilities

Use an authorised company device and protect the workspace login, local files, API credentials and authorisation codes. Do not share tokens, upload the private data folder to public repositories, disable certificate checks or use real customer data for the generated sandbox delivery tests. Review imported values against QuickBooks before relying on them for financial work. Report suspected unauthorised access or an incorrect connection to the operator promptly.

4. Data and external services

The accompanying privacy notice describes the connector's processing and storage. Intuit, Billit and GitHub provide separate services governed by their applicable terms and privacy notices. A sandbox delivery result is not a certification of tax treatment or production e-invoicing compliance.

5. Ending access

The operator may withdraw internal permission to use the connector. Users can remove the local live connection in the app and separately revoke the Intuit grant in QuickBooks' connected-app controls. Removing the connection does not delete imported local records or retained audit entries. Company data-handling instructions continue to apply to retained files and backups.

6. Scope and changes

The connector supports internal review and testing; it does not replace accounting judgement, statutory records, tax advice or company security policies. These terms describe the current functionality. The operator should review them when functionality, authorised use or data handling changes.
