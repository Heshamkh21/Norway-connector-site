Connection controls

Disconnect QuickBooks

Remove the saved local connection and revoke app access in QuickBooks when needed.

Remove the local live connection

Start the Norway app on your computer, then open its connection controls.

Open local connection controls
  1. Sign in to the workspace if prompted, then open Connection settings → Live Norway company.
  2. Expand Manage live connection.
  3. Click Remove local live connection.

This removes saved Production tokens and company confirmation from the installed app. Its existing sandbox connection and imported local document history are retained.

Revoke access at QuickBooks too

To revoke the Intuit authorization itself, sign in to the connected Norway company in QuickBooks, open its apps or connected-app management, find this connector and choose Disconnect. Intuit's menu names may vary.

If you have already disconnected through QuickBooks, remove the local saved connection as described above.

The local remove button does not call Intuit's revocation API. Imported local copies remain until you delete them in the app. Deleting a local copy does not delete the original in QuickBooks.

Opening this page does not disconnect anything. Connection changes happen only through the controls you use in QuickBooks or the authenticated local app.
