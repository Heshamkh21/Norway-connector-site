What the connector reads

The live connection reads the selected company's identity and preferences, then imports invoices or credit notes for a date range. Original document numbers, dates, customer fields, currencies, lines, tax and totals are kept for comparison.

QuickBooks offers a broad accounting permission. The connector enforces production accounting reads only. Separately generated sandbox fixtures remain available for explicit Billit and Peppol sandbox testing.
